Vehicle Ownership Experience

Services Overview

Rosmerta Digital's Vehicle Ownership Experience redefines convenience and efficiency in vehicle registration and compliance solutions. Our comprehensive suite of services is designed to cater to the diverse needs of modern vehicle owners and businesses

#1

Vehicle Registration

Streamlined registration processes for both new and
pre-owned vehicles, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements while minimising administrative burdens for owners and dealers alike.

Our Vehicle Registration services are tailored to cater to the following target segments:

New-age D2C
E2W OEMs

Innovative solutions designed to meet the unique needs of direct-to-consumer electric two-wheeler manufacturers, facilitating efficient registration and compliance processes for their cutting-edge vehicles.

OEMs selling to
corporates

Tailored services to support original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) in their dealings with corporate clients, ensuring seamless registration and compliance solutions for fleet purchases and corporate vehicle acquisitions.

Companies dealing
in Used cars

Comprehensive support for businesses engaged in the sale and purchase of pre-owned vehicles, streamlining registration processes and compliance requirements to enhance operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Examples of Brands Served

#2

FAME Subsidy Processing

Expert handling of documentation and processing for Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Electric Vehicles (FAME) subsidies, facilitating incentives for electric vehicle adoption and promoting sustainable mobility solutions.

#3

State Transport Approval

Efficient coordination and processing of approvals from state transport authorities, ensuring compliance with regional regulations and facilitating smooth vehicle operations across different states.

#4

Trade Certificate / Trade License

Simplified procedures for obtaining trade certificates and licences, essential for dealerships and businesses involved in vehicle sales and trade.

#5

State Subsidy Processing

Dedicated assistance in processing subsidies offered by state governments, helping businesses and individuals leverage available incentives for vehicle purchases and operations.

At Rosmerta Digital, we are committed to delivering excellence in vehicle ownership experience services, empowering individuals and businesses to navigate the complexities of vehicle registration and compliance with confidence and ease. Experience the difference with Rosmerta Digital today.
