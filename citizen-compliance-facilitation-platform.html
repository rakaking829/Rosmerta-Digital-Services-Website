Citizen Compliance Facilitation Platform

  • Citizen Compliance Facilitation Platform

A Smarter Way to Manage Vehicle Compliance.

Rosmerta brings together technology, mobility infrastructure, government-connected services and operational expertise to build a more connected compliance ecosystem.

Rosmerta's E-Challan Module is a complete compliance closure engine, not merely a payment layer. We own the entire challan lifecycle end to end, from automatic discovery to verified closure.

A smarter way to enforce. A seamless way to comply, powering India's dealer-led citizen compliance ecosystem with technology-first solutions built for scale, speed and transparency.

Aligned with Rule 167 Modernisation (G.S.R. 48(E), 2026), the landmark statutory framework that transforms challan compliance from voluntary behaviour into a structured, enforceable infrastructure workflow.

A single QR or link takes any citizen from challan discovery to verified closure, at a showroom, service desk or delivery counter; making compliance instant, transparent and effortless.

One Platform. The Complete Compliance Journey.

Challan Discovery

Identify pending challans across all vehicles automatically surfaced accurately without manual intervention.

Payment

Secure PCI-DSS compliant digital payment with instant confirmation - fast, safe and reliable.

Dispute Management

Route contested cases with required documentation and evidence, portal-based contesting with automated routing to state-designated authority.

Court & Rto

Case packet preparation, hearing follow-up and NTT de-flagging, every offline resolution managed end to end.

Closure

Verified receipts, case outcomes and closure proof tracked and stored permanently for every case.

Analytics & Visibility

Compliance activity, real-time status, SLA adherence and reporting, all consolidated in one view.
