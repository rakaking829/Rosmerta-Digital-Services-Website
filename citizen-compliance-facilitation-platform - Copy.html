Automotive Spare Parts & Components

  • Home
  • Digitally Enabled Channel Sales > Our Spares Range

Driving businesses forward with genuine parts, trusted brands and dependable global sourcing.

Rosmerta Automotive Spares offers a comprehensive portfolio of genuine and aftermarket automotive components for cars, LCVs, HCVs, two-wheelers, three-wheelers and tractors.

We bring together products from leading automotive manufacturers and established aftermarket brands, providing access to a broad selection of components through our distribution network. Our portfolio includes recognised names across passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, two-wheelers, automotive components and lubricants.

We focus on offering genuine automotive products at competitive prices, helping customers source the components they need while supporting their business and operational requirements. Our broad product portfolio enables customers to access multiple product categories through a single sourcing partner.

With an export presence spanning over 80 countries, Rosmerta Digital Services connects Indian automotive products with international markets. Our growing global network reflects our commitment to expanding market access and building lasting business relationships across geographies.

We prioritise dependable service, responsive support and delivery options tailored to customer requirements. Backed by a broad product portfolio and a customer-focused approach, we work to simplify automotive spare parts sourcing for businesses across markets.
