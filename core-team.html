Independent Directors

Ms. Sunita Trivedi

Independent Director

Ms. Sunita Trivedi

Independent Director

Ms. Sunita Trivedi is currently serving as the Director of Rosmerta Digital. She has carved a distinguished career path marked by her leadership in public service and corporate governance. With over 35 years of experience, culminating in the top rank of Member Postal Services Board, her expertise extends across financial management, marketing, HR, estate management, procurement, and modernization initiatives. Trivedi’s tenure includes significant contributions such as spearheading the Business Development Directorate for the Government of India, playing a pivotal role in the nationwide introduction of Aadhar Cards, and establishing the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Scheme. Her adept handling of the National Census logistics, leadership in government insurance (PLI), and the successful launch of India Post Banks further underscore her impactful legacy in the realms of public administration and corporate governance.

Mr. Ashok Kacker

Independent Director

Mr. Ashok Kacker

Independent Director

Mr. Ashok Kacker is a former Indian Revenue Service (IRS) officer with over three decades of experience. He has held senior positions in both executive and policy-making capacities, including Chief Commissioner of Income Tax and Executive Director at SEBI. Known for his dynamic leadership and ability to drive organizational growth, he excels in finance, taxation, and capital markets. Currently, he is the Founder and Managing Partner of A.K. He holds a Master’s degree in Physics from the University of Allahabad and has served on various prestigious boards including Max India Limited and Mahindra Satyam.

Mr. Dilip Chenoy

Independent Director

Mr. Dilip Chenoy

Independent Director

Mr. Dilip Chenoy is a seasoned leader with extensive experience across various industries. He served as Secretary General at the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce & Industry (FICCI) from 2018 to 2021, where he led significant change management efforts and enhanced the organization’s brand. Due to his exceptional leadership and strategic vision, he is on the Board of Directors of Rosmerta Technologies. Prior to this, he was the Managing Director & CEO of the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), where he played a pivotal role in building India’s skill development ecosystem. His earlier roles include Director General at the Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM) and Deputy Director General at the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII). With a strong background in policy formulation and industry development, Mr. Chenoy has been instrumental in in numerous initiatives and partnerships, both domestically and internationally.

Mr. Balraj Bhanot

Technical Consultant

Technical Consultant
