Vehicle Ownership Experience

Advantage To OEMs

At Rosmerta Digital, we revolutionise the vehicle ownership experience for Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) through our innovative platform, "Urja." Our journey begins at the critical point-of-purchase, seamlessly integrating into the OEM customer experience to streamline the vehicle registration process.

Key Features and Advantages of the Urja Platform :

#1

Deep Integration with
OEM Systems

Urja is deeply integrated with OEMs' internal systems, ensuring that all processes are synchronised and data flows seamlessly between platforms. This integration facilitates efficient management of customer information and registration data.

#2

Robust Safety and Compliance Measures

The platform incorporates multiple checks and balances to guarantee a safe and compliant vehicle acquisition and registration process. This ensures that all legal and regulatory requirements are met, providing peace of mind for both customers and OEMs.

#3

Real-Time Status Updates

Urja provides live updates on the current order status to customers, OEMs, and registration authorities. This transparency allows all parties to stay informed about the progress of the registration, reducing uncertainties and improving communication.

#4

Customizable Platform Stages

The platform’s stages are fully customizable to align with state-specific compliance mechanisms and OEM-specific use case requirements. This flexibility ensures that Urja can adapt to various regulatory environments and business needs, making it a versatile solution for OEMs across different regions.

Enhancing the Vehicle Ownership Experience

By integrating Urja into the OEM customer journey, Rosmerta Digital enhances the overall vehicle ownership experience. Our platform not only simplifies the registration process but also ensures compliance, safety, and transparency at every step. OEMs can now offer their customers a seamless, efficient, and reliable service, reinforcing their commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.
