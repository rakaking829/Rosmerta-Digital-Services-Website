About The Group

Rosmerta (ROS-MUR-TA) stands as a beacon of innovation in India's mobility solutions landscape. With a steadfast commitment to excellence and a rich history of groundbreaking advancements, Rosmerta has become a driving force in automotive technology. Rooted in a vision to create safer, smarter, and more connected Indian roads, the company is at the forefront of the Surakshit Safar initiative dedicated to reducing road accidents and fatalities by 50 percent by 2030.

Rosmerta holds a remarkable 60% market share in the Indian High-Security Registration Plate (HSRP) Industry, making it the world's second-largest and India's foremost manufacturer.

Rosmerta excels in operating across diverse domains: Identification, Sustainability, Connected Electronics, Compliance Services, and Vehicle Care. The group is committed to delivering innovative solutions and making a significant impact across the automotive industry.

Rosmerta's offerings demonstrate a dedication to transforming India's roads through cutting-edge technology, providing comprehensive solutions for a smarter and safer transportation ecosystem.
