Advisory Team

Balraj Bhanot

Mr. Balraj Bhanot is a double Graduate Engineer with Post Graduate Diploma in Industrial Management and MBA from FMS Delhi apart from Quality Management Degree from SIDA, Sweden. His professional career spans over 60 years, half of which was in the field of Electronics and balance half in Automobile. He Retired as Dy. Director General , M/O Heavy Industries and was former Director ARAI,Pune-a premier Homologation Body in India. He was Chairman CMVR TSC (MORTH), Chairman AISC (MORTH), Chairman TEDC (BIS).He is a homologation stalwart having Established the rules in the country from Scratch. He also served as Adviser Electronics in India Trade Centre in Indian Mission to EEC. Additionally, he has been an Adviser for DIMTS, CSE, IRTE, and Chairman of TED 28 & 31 (BIS) besides host of Industry.He has pioneering experience of setting Vehicle Inspection and Certification Centers in the country and Running HSRP project since inception.

Neeraj Mathur

Mr. Neeraj Mathur is a leading automotive professional with 42 years of experience in sourcing management, business strategy, and relationship management. He has worked with prominent companies such as Maruti Suzuki, GM, Delphi, and Hero MotoCorp Limited. Throughout his career, Mr. Mathur has led business restructuring and cost transformation initiatives. He is respected in the automotive industry for his down-to-earth attitude and recognized as a thought leader.

Vijay Sethi

Advisor

Vijay Sethi is an advisor at Rosmerta Digital with over 30 years of experience spanning the manufacturing industry and consulting environment. He is a proven leader with specialization in Digital Transformation, Large Scale IT projects, Cyber security, Community Development Projects, Talent Development and Employee Engagement. This expertise is further complemented by his prestigious past roles as Chairman of both the IT committee and the CSR committee of Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM), showcasing his well-rounded proficiency in technology, business, and social responsibility.
