Board of Directors

Mukesh Malhotra

Directors

Mr. Mukesh currently serves as the Director of Rosmerta Digital. He brings a wealth of experience to this role, overseeing both strategic planning and business operations for the company. Renowned for his expertise in the automotive industry, he boasts over 26 years of experience working in key positions across various conglomerates within pharmaceuticals and telecommunications. Mr. Mukesh possesses a multifaceted skillset encompassing strategic planning, financial management, team leadership, and marketing. Notably, his deep understanding of domestic and global market dynamics, coupled with his proven ability to forge strong relationships with stakeholders, customers, and partners, positions him as a valuable asset to the organization.

Suneeta Trivedi

Directors

Currently serving as the Director of Rosmerta Digital, Suneeta Trivedi has carved a distinguished career path marked by her leadership in public service and corporate governance. With over 35 years of experience, culminating in the top rank of Member Postal Services Board, her expertise extends across financial management, marketing, HR, estate management, procurement, and modernization initiatives. Trivedi's tenure includes significant contributions such as spearheading the Business Development Directorate for the Government of India, playing a pivotal role in the nationwide introduction of Aadhar Cards, and establishing the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Scheme. Her adept handling of the National Census logistics, leadership in government insurance (PLI), and the successful launch of India Post Banks further underscore her impactful legacy in the realms of public administration and corporate governance.
