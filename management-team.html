Management Team

Akhil Gupta

Chief Executive Officer

Akhil is a seasoned and multidisciplinary business strategist and leader with over 20 years of rich and progressive experience in the financial services industry. As the current CEO of Rosmerta Digital Services Limited, he drives the company's mission to revolutionize vehicle registration and automotive services in India. Akhil's career includes pivotal roles in esteemed organizations such as Indiabulls, Moser Baer, Ramco, ICICI Bank, and Luminous. His leadership, characterized by galvanizing high-performing teams and exceeding performance standards, is instrumental in steering Rosmerta Digital towards innovation and operational excellence.

Sanjay Sharma

Head Spares

Mr. Sanjay Sharma, Head of Spares at Rosmerta digital services, is a distinguished professional with over 31 years of experience, specializing in Business Development, Sales & Marketing, and Strategic Alliances. He has demonstrated remarkable leadership in high-performance team management, strategic initiatives, and business growth. His extensive career includes significant tenures at DCM Data Systems, Siemens Information Systems Limited, Microsoft India, Star Services, Sprinklr India Private Limited, and Rezo.AI, where he consistently delivered exceptional results. Sanjay's core competencies encompass P&L management, cost optimization, process development, product management, channel development, and key account management. His strategic vision, innovative thinking, and unwavering commitment to excellence have established him as a visionary leader in the industry, driving sustainable growth and fostering strategic partnerships.

Sudhanshu

Chief Technology Officer

Mr. Sudhanshu, Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Rosmerta Digital services, brings an impressive 23-year career spanning big conglomerates, the IT industry, banking and finance, and the automotive sector. An IIT Delhi alumnus, he has made significant contributions to companies such as Sunlife Finance, RBS, AMEX, Interrs Software, and Patni Computers. His expertise includes global service management, migration, software initiatives, and design and development. Known for his leadership in managing complex IT environments, Mr. Sudhanshu excels in orchestrating onshore and offshore collaborations in a matrix environment. His strategic vision and technical acumen have driven substantial advancements in IT infrastructure and processes, ensuring high performance and customer satisfaction across diverse sectors.

Ajay Kumar Singh

Chief Human Resource Officer

Mr. Ajay Kumar Singh, the Head of Human Resources at Rosmerta digital services, is a management graduate with 25 years of HR experience. He carries over twenty-five years of rich experience and has worked with organizations like Cyber Soft Technology Private Limited, Orient Group, Shahi Exports Private Limited, Voith Paper Fabrics India Limited, Roop Group, and Toyoda Gosei Minda India Private Limited. His expertise includes Strategic Management, Conceptualisation of HR Policies, Establishing HR processes, budgeting, talent acquisition, performance management, statutory compliance, and employee engagement He is skilled in ensuring talent availability, recruitment, change management, and enhancing employee morale.

Kuntal Kar

Company Secretary

Mr. Kuntal Kar is the Group Company Secretary and Head of Legal at Rosmerta Digital services, a position he has held since December 2022. With over two decades of experience, he excels in managing legal, compliance, investor services, litigation, and corporate governance functions. At Rosmerta, he oversees secretarial and investor relations for 30+ companies, manages board meetings, ensures compliance with Companies Act regulations, and handles a treasury corpus of over INR 50 Crores. Previously, he held significant roles at ASSOCHAM, Unitech Limited, PG Electroplast Limited, Cherry Hill Interiors Limited, Pearson Education, Lloyd Insulations (I) Limited, and Cressanda Solutions Ltd. Mr. Kar's expertise includes secretarial, compliance, contract management, commercial negotiation, FCRA compliance, financial planning, and strategic planning. He is a law graduate from CCS University, Meerut, holds an MBA in Finance from International Management Institute, New Delhi, and is a qualified member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India.
